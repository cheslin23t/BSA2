About this demo

BSA2 was initially built for my Boy Scout troop’s website and served as its primary trip RSVP. The original Flask app accepted a scout’s response, stored it in Replit’s key/value database, and split the organizer roster into going and not-going lists.

What is preserved

  • The original Nicepage-era black, gray, orange, and outdoor-photo visual design.
  • The home, trip signup, admin login, and organizer roster sequence.
  • The form fields and the going/not-going grouping used by the Flask routes.

What changed for safety

  • No server, live troop calendar, or Replit database is connected.
  • Demo responses remain in this browser’s local storage.
  • The visible demo credentials are not real authentication.
  • Only fictional sample people and 555 phone numbers are included.
